Day 1 Tips

  • Do chest first while you're fresh, triceps after
  • Superset rope pushdowns with overhead extensions for a brutal finisher
  • Rest 90s for compounds, 60s for isolation
  • Close grip bench doubles as chest + tricep — very efficient

  • Slow negatives (3 sec down) on curls for maximum growth
